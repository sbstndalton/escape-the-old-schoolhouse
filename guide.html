Escape the Old Schoolhouse

Quick-start guide for teachers

Make an escape room for your class: students solve short puzzles about your chapter, then type the exit password from their certificate into a quiz. Keep this page beside your screen.

Start here: https://sbstndalton.github.io/escape-the-old-schoolhouse/

It fits on one page, on Letter or A4 paper.

Seven steps

  1. Open the maker

    Go to the address above and press Make an escape room. You don't need an account or anything to install.

  2. Follow the steps (about 30 to 45 minutes)

    The maker asks one question per page, in 10 steps. Choose Change the sample or Start with an empty escape room, then press Continue on each page. Stuck? Open Help with this step. To stop, press Save and finish later.

  3. Check your facts

    On Check your facts, compare each item with your textbook, fill in Where you checked it, and tick I checked this is correct. Until every item is ticked, students see a “Draft” notice. The sample was written with AI help, so check every item from it.

  4. Try it as a student

    Press Start the student view and play it. Show answers lets you skip ahead. Press Esc or Exit preview to come back.

  1. Share a class link

    Choose Share a class link (recommended), press Make the class link, then Copy link. Paste it into Moodle, Canvas, Google Classroom or an email; the maker shows where it goes in Moodle and Canvas.

    The link holds the escape room as it is now. Changed something? Press Make a new class link and share the new one: the old link keeps the old version.

  2. Make the one-question exit-password quiz

    The grade comes from a quiz in your course with one question:

    Type the exit password from the certificate at the end of the escape room.

    The answer is your exit password (the maker shows it, with a Copy button). Make the quiz ignore capital letters. In Moodle: a Short answer question, Case sensitivity: No, case is unimportant. In Canvas: a Fill in the Blank question with Close enough and Spelling only (ignore case), or add the password in small letters as a second answer. Menus can differ by version.

  3. Keep your backup

    Press Download a backup and keep the file somewhere safe, such as your course folder. It plays as the game in any browser and opens again in the maker. Your class link is a backup too.

Your work is saved in this browser only

It stays in this browser, on this computer. Another browser or computer won't have it, and clearing your browsing history removes it. So keep your backup file or your class link.

If something goes wrong

My work is gone, or I'm on a new computer
Open the maker and choose Open one I saved before. Then press Choose the file and pick your backup, or paste your class link.
A student says the link doesn't open
If it says “This class link is incomplete”, part of it was cut off. Copy it again with Copy link and paste all of it. If it keeps happening, choose I'll email or post a file instead.
I want to change the exit password
Press Show all settings (Advanced mode), open Finish & grading, and change Exit password. Then make a new class link. In your quiz, keep the old password as a second correct answer.

Nothing you write is sent anywhere. No accounts, no tracking, no cookies. The accessibility statement is linked from the front page.
