How it works

  1. You make itType your questions, step by step. It is saved in your browser as you go.
  2. You share a class linkPaste it into Moodle, Canvas, Google Classroom or an email.
  3. Students type the exit password into your quizThe password is on their certificate. Your course records the grade.

Privacy: nothing is sent anywhere. The escape room travels inside the link itself: the part of a class link after the “#” never reaches a web server. There are no accounts, no tracking and no cookies.
